DESTINATION 05 / 07 · CENTRAL COAST

Umina / Ettalong

An easy drive, beach mornings and low-effort family outings.

Umina and Ettalong make a practical short-break base: the beach, playground and meals can form most of the holiday. Add a ferry trip or surf lesson only if the group wants an extra adventure.

1¼–1¾ hrs from Summer HillKids 6–13
Explore the highlights ↓
☀

Our pick: Start with Umina Beach and the Peninsula playground. Ettalong’s foreshore gives everyone an easy change of scene.

FIVE IDEAS · PICK WHAT SUITS THE CREW

What we could get up to.

Time allowances and family-fit suggestions are our planning judgement. Check current opening hours, conditions and operator requirements before committing.

01

Umina Beach

Beach outing

Allow a morning

Keep the plan simple: sand play, beach games and a swim in the patrolled area when available. This is the easy everyday option when everyone would prefer less time in the car.

For our crew A good mixed-age outing when swimming plans match ability and conditions.

Before we go Check flags and patrols, supervise closely and bring shade and drinking water.

02

Peninsula Recreation Precinct

Playground outing

Allow 1–2 hours

Give the younger kids a playground session and make it a picnic stop for the whole crew. It works well as a break between beach time and an afternoon back at the accommodation.

For our crew Particularly handy for the younger children in the group.

Before we go Check facilities, weather and shade; keep an eye on children around nearby roads and water.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
03

Ettalong foreshore & cafés

Walk; food extra

Allow 1–2 hours

A foreshore wander and an easy meal can fill an afternoon without another booking. Let the families take a slower pace while still getting out to see a different part of the peninsula.

For our crew An easy all-age choice with room to stop whenever needed.

Before we go Pick a meeting point and check the actual beach conditions before adding a swim.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
04

A ferry outing to Palm Beach

Paid fares

Allow half a day

Turn the crossing into the activity: take the ferry from Ettalong for a change of scenery, then return with enough time to avoid a rushed evening. Keep the destination plan modest.

For our crew A fun mixed-age outing for kids happy on boats.

Before we go Check the current timetable, return sailing, fares and weather; services can change.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
05

Cinema Paradiso

Paid tickets

Allow a film session

Keep this as the wet-weather backup rather than a compulsory item. A film in Ettalong can give the kids a break from the heat or rain and the adults an easy group outing.

For our crew Choose the film for the youngest child and check the rating.

Before we go Check session times and book enough seats for everyone.

A LOOSE DAY PLAN

One outing is enough.

  1. Morning: Umina Beach in suitable conditions.
  2. Lunch: a picnic and play at Peninsula Recreation Precinct.
  3. Afternoon: Ettalong foreshore, an easy meal or time back at the accommodation.

Use this for one full day on either date option. It is a suggested rhythm, not a booked itinerary.

IF THE WEATHER CHANGES

Keep a plan B.

Cinema Paradiso in Ettalong is a useful rainy-day option. Check sessions, film ratings and seats for the whole group.

THE PRACTICAL BIT

Before we go.

  • Umina and Ettalong are nearby but separate places; choose your accommodation base first.
  • A ferry outing needs a current timetable and a plan for the return trip.
  • Holiday-resort programmes vary; do not assume January 2027 activities are already confirmed.
