DESTINATION 03 / 07 · NORTH COAST

Port Stephens

Dolphins, giant dunes and a different beach every day.

Port Stephens suits families who want a little more organised adventure. Cruises and dune tours can be memorable shared outings; balance them with simple bay-beach time so the trip does not become a string of bookings.

2¾–3½ hrs from Summer HillKids 6–13

FIVE IDEAS · PICK WHAT SUITS THE CREW

What we could get up to.

Time allowances and family-fit suggestions are our planning judgement. Check current opening hours, conditions and operator requirements before committing.

01

Dolphin-watching cruise

Paid cruise

Allow half a day with check-in

A cruise from Nelson Bay gives the whole crew one shared adventure on the water. Choose a family-suitable boat and route, and leave space for a meal or beach stop afterwards.

For our crew A mixed-age option if everyone is comfortable on a boat.

Before we go Check child fares, boarding time, weather changes and seasickness considerations; sightings are not guaranteed.

02

Sandboarding on the dunes

Paid tour

Allow half a day

Join an organised tour into the Stockton dunes in the Worimi Conservation Lands. It is a memorable way for energetic kids to do something completely different from swimming.

For our crew Best for kids comfortable with climbing sand hills; check minimum ages.

Before we go Book with an operator, confirm transport and restrictions, and bring water and sun protection.

03

Shoal Bay beach day

Beach outing

Allow a morning

Use Shoal Bay for the simple part of the holiday: beach games, a picnic and time by the bay. Choose the actual swimming spot after checking the day’s weather and water conditions.

For our crew Good for mixed ages when the adults match the outing to conditions.

Before we go Check patrols and local swimming advice; sheltered bays still need supervision.

04

Tomaree Head views

No tour ticket

Allow 1½–2 hours for the walk

An optional adventure for the fitter part of the group. The summit route is short in distance but steep, so make it a separate choice rather than expecting every family to join.

For our crew Optional for fit older kids and adults; not our default plan for six-year-olds.

Before we go NSW National Parks grades this as a challenging Grade 5 walk. Check alerts, heat and footwear before deciding.

Beach & pool downtime

Depends on your stay

Allow an afternoon

Build in a deliberately empty afternoon. A beach near your base or facilities at the chosen holiday park gives everyone a break between the bigger outings and keeps the adults’ driving manageable.

For our crew Useful for the younger kids and anyone who wants a slower pace.

A LOOSE DAY PLAN

One outing is enough.

  1. Morning: a pre-booked dolphin cruise from Nelson Bay.
  2. Lunch: picnic or an easy waterfront meal.
  3. Afternoon: beach time at Shoal Bay in suitable conditions, then a relaxed shared dinner.

Use this for one full day on either date option. It is a suggested rhythm, not a booked itinerary.

IF THE WEATHER CHANGES

Keep a plan B.

Swap outdoor plans for a relaxed meal and games at the accommodation. Check the park’s indoor facilities; do not assume a cruise or dune tour will run in bad weather.

THE PRACTICAL BIT

Before we go.

  • Nelson Bay, Shoal Bay and Anna Bay are distinct bases; allow local driving.
  • Dolphin sightings are not guaranteed, and January is outside the usual whale-migration season.
  • Skip the Fingal sand-spit crossing. For walks, assess heat, fitness and the youngest child’s needs.
