DESTINATION 04 / 07 · SOUTH COAST

Jervis Bay

White-sand beaches, gentle walks and wildlife on the water.

The appeal here is the water and scenery. Use Huskisson or Vincentia as an easy base, pick one cruise or paddle, and spend the rest of the time exploring beaches without trying to visit all of them.

3–3½ hrs from Summer HillKids 6–13
Explore the highlights ↓
☀

Our pick: A short White Sands Walk plus one beach stop is a strong starting point. Add a dolphin cruise if the group wants a booked activity.

FIVE IDEAS · PICK WHAT SUITS THE CREW

What we could get up to.

Time allowances and family-fit suggestions are our planning judgement. Check current opening hours, conditions and operator requirements before committing.

01

White Sands Walk

Park charges may apply

Allow 1–2 hours

Walk a manageable section through coastal forest and stop to admire the bright sand and bay views. Starting around Greenfield Beach lets the group combine a short walk with a picnic rather than a long hike.

For our crew Good for mixed ages when you choose a shorter section.

Before we go Check the route, park alerts and parking; allow extra time for beach stops.

02

Murrays Beach in Booderee

Park entry charges

Allow half a day

Set aside a proper beach morning in Booderee National Park. Murrays is an appealing place for sand, scenery and a swim or snorkel when conditions and ability suit.

For our crew A family beach outing; snorkelling is for confident swimmers under supervision.

Before we go Check park entry, alerts, parking and swimming conditions. Do not assume every beach is patrolled.

03

Huskisson dolphin cruise

Paid cruise

Allow half a day with check-in

A dolphin-watching cruise makes a useful shared activity without filling the whole trip with appointments. Huskisson also gives the families somewhere easy to eat before or after boarding.

For our crew Mixed ages if the kids are comfortable on a boat.

Before we go Check child fares, weather policy and departure details; wildlife sightings cannot be promised.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
04

Family kayaking or SUP

Paid hire or tour

Allow half a day

Arrange a paddle suited to the group rather than hiring first and improvising a route. It is a good option for families who would enjoy a little activity between beach days.

For our crew Ask the operator about doubles, child ages and swimming requirements.

Before we go Confirm the launch point, lifejackets, supervision and wind forecast.

05

Jervis Bay Maritime Museum

Check admission

Allow 1–2 hours

Add a history stop at the museum in Huskisson when everyone wants something different from sand and swimming. It can also be a useful alternative when the weather changes.

For our crew Good for curious kids; keep the visit short enough for the youngest.

Before we go Check opening hours, current exhibits and admission before going.

A LOOSE DAY PLAN

One outing is enough.

  1. Morning: a short stretch of the White Sands Walk.
  2. Lunch: picnic at a chosen beach, keeping parking and shade in mind.
  3. Afternoon: downtime back at the accommodation or a pre-arranged family paddle.

Use this for one full day on either date option. It is a suggested rhythm, not a booked itinerary.

IF THE WEATHER CHANGES

Keep a plan B.

Check the Jervis Bay Maritime Museum’s opening hours for a change of scene, then keep a café stop and cabin games in reserve.

THE PRACTICAL BIT

Before we go.

  • Hyams Beach parking is limited in summer; keep alternatives around Vincentia in mind.
  • Booderee has its own entry requirements and park alerts, so check before driving in.
  • Bay beaches can be windy and unpatrolled; check conditions and supervise children.
