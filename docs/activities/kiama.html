DESTINATION 02 / 07 · SOUTH COAST

Kiama

Ocean pools, blowholes and adventures just inland.

Kiama is a good option when different families want different things: a town wander, a beach morning or a bigger ticketed outing. Pick one headline adventure and use the coastline for everything in between.

1¾–2¼ hrs from Summer HillKids 6–13
Explore the highlights ↓
☀

Our pick: The blowhole and a short coastal stroll make an easy first outing. Choose either rainforest time or Jamberoo for the bigger day.

FIVE IDEAS · PICK WHAT SUITS THE CREW

What we could get up to.

Time allowances and family-fit suggestions are our planning judgement. Check current opening hours, conditions and operator requirements before committing.

01

Kiama Blowhole & lighthouse

No attraction ticket

Allow 1–2 hours

Start with Kiama’s best-known natural spectacle, then explore the lighthouse headland and harbour. A picnic gives the kids space to pause while everyone gets their bearings.

For our crew A straightforward all-age outing with adults keeping children behind barriers.

Before we go The blowhole’s spray varies with sea conditions; stay on marked paths.

02

Blowhole Point Ocean Pool

Swimming outing

Allow 1–2 hours

A pool stop can make a coastal walk feel like a proper summer outing. Blowhole Point has a rock-floor pool and a wading area, but the sea still determines whether it is a suitable place to swim.

For our crew Choose the visit around swimming ability and conditions, with close supervision.

Before we go Check swell, access and local advice; enclosed ocean water is not risk-free.

03

A bite-sized Coast Walk

No tour ticket

Allow 1–2 hours

Choose a short out-and-back stretch for ocean views, a beach stop and perhaps the Little Blowhole. There is no need to tackle the full coastal route with six-year-olds in summer.

For our crew Works for mixed ages when the adults choose a manageable section.

Before we go Bring water and sun protection, and check route length and access before starting.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
04

Minnamurra Rainforest

Park charges may apply

Allow half a day

Head inland for a change of scenery. The rainforest loop offers boardwalks, bridges and wildlife-spotting; it is a gentler nature plan than making a long waterfall hike the main event.

For our crew A good option for interested younger walkers; assess mobility and fitness.

Before we go Check park alerts, the route and access charges. Longer falls trails add distance and effort.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
05

Jamberoo Action Park

Paid tickets

Allow a full day

This is the bigger splash-and-slide day for families who want a headline attraction. Plan it as a separate outing, with time for breaks and different rides for different ages.

For our crew Especially appealing to older kids; younger children need age-appropriate options.

Before we go Confirm January operating dates, tickets, ride height rules and supervision requirements.

A LOOSE DAY PLAN

One outing is enough.

  1. Morning: lighthouse and blowhole, followed by a short coastal stroll.
  2. Lunch: harbour picnic or a family meal in town.
  3. Afternoon: an ocean-pool visit if conditions suit, then time back at the accommodation.

Use this for one full day on either date option. It is a suggested rhythm, not a booked itinerary.

IF THE WEATHER CHANGES

Keep a plan B.

Keep cafés and cabin games as the simplest fallback. If you want an indoor swim, check Kiama Leisure Centre’s current opening hours before going.

THE PRACTICAL BIT

Before we go.

  • Stay behind the fences at the blowhole and cliffs; water plumes depend on the swell.
  • Ocean pools can be affected by rough seas; check conditions before entering.
  • Jamberoo is a separate outing with tickets and ride restrictions, not an automatic activity for every child.
