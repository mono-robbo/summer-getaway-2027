DESTINATION 01 / 07 · SOUTH COAST

Gerroa / Shoalhaven Heads

Beach, river and a little country-town wandering.

A strong choice when the three families want one relaxed base. Give the kids beach time, choose one lesson or short outing, and leave the afternoon free for the pool and a shared dinner.

2–3 hrs from Summer HillKids 6–13

Our pick: Start with Seven Mile Beach and one surf lesson. Berry is a useful change of scene if the crew wants a break from the sand.

FIVE IDEAS · PICK WHAT SUITS THE CREW

What we could get up to.

Time allowances and family-fit suggestions are our planning judgement. Check current opening hours, conditions and operator requirements before committing.

01

Seven Mile Beach

Beach outing

Allow a morning

Make this the easy default: sandcastles, beach cricket and a swim in suitable conditions. Keep the day loose enough for the younger kids to rest and the older ones to keep playing.

For our crew An easy mixed-age choice with close adult supervision.

Before we go Use patrolled areas when available; check surf and parking conditions.

02

A beginner surf lesson

Paid lesson

Allow half a day with setup

Turn one morning into the trip’s shared adventure. Gerroa has surf-school options on Seven Mile Beach, so the adults can organise a lesson rather than bringing all the equipment.

For our crew Good for interested kids; the operator must confirm age and swimming requirements.

Before we go Check minimum ages, lesson availability, equipment inclusions and weather policy.

03

Crooked River picnic

Picnic; hire extra

Allow 1–2 hours

Settle in by the river for lunch and scenery. If paddling appeals, arrange suitable equipment or an operator-supported outing rather than treating the river mouth as a casual swimming spot.

For our crew Good as a picnic for all ages; paddling depends on conditions and ability.

Before we go Tides and currents change. Confirm local conditions, safe launch points and lifejackets.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
04

Berry & Boongaree Nature Play Park

Playground outing

Allow half a day

Swap towels for a country-town outing: a playground stop at Boongaree, a wander through Berry and a family lunch. It is an easy way to give the youngest kids a different kind of adventure.

For our crew Particularly useful for the younger end of the 6–13 group.

Before we go Allow a local drive from your base; check weather, facilities and café hours.

Official information ↗Map & location ↗
05

A short national-park walk

No tour ticket

Allow 1–2 hours

Choose a short walk through the coastal forest or a beachside picnic in Seven Mile Beach National Park. Make it a nature-spotting outing rather than a distance challenge.

For our crew Flexible for mixed ages: turn back before the younger kids run out of energy.

Before we go Check park alerts and the chosen route; bring water, hats and insect protection. Parking charges may apply.

A LOOSE DAY PLAN

One outing is enough.

  1. Morning: beach games and a patrolled swim where available.
  2. Lunch: picnic near the river or an easy café stop.
  3. Afternoon: a short nature walk or downtime at the holiday park.

Use this for one full day on either date option. It is a suggested rhythm, not a booked itinerary.

IF THE WEATHER CHANGES

Keep a plan B.

Try a café-and-shops outing in Berry or Gerringong, then games back at the cabins. Check opening hours before heading out.

THE PRACTICAL BIT

Before we go.

  • Gerroa and Shoalhaven Heads are separate bases; keep the three families in one of them.
  • Check beach patrols, tides and river-mouth currents. Sheltered-looking water is not automatically safe.
  • Book a lesson for the actual ages and abilities in your group, rather than assuming every session accepts six-year-olds.
