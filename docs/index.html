BEFORE WE BOOKHow the budget estimates work

Cabin allowances

Each range is for one family cabin per night, assuming two adults and two children. Coastal ranges are our provisional summer planning allowances, not a verified January price survey. Kangaroo Valley’s range is informed by Glenmack’s published peak tariffs, including two children’s charges. Rates may fall outside these ranges.

The number of cabins needed depends on who comes. Adjoining cabins, guest surcharges, fees and minimum stays need checking directly.

Airbnb house allowances

Each range is for one entire home, with at least four bedrooms and enough permitted capacity for the confirmed group. These are budgets we propose for the search, not advertised prices for the example homes. Work out each family’s share once the participating families are confirmed.

The examples have 4–6 bedrooms and advertise at least 12 guests; that does not mean they will fit the final group. Bedroom counts and capacities were checked on 14 October 2026; beds, layout, adult limits and availability still need confirming.

Fees & short stays

Our figures exclude cleaning, service fees, taxes, extra-guest charges and refundable bonds. Airbnb’s selected-date total normally includes fees, so compare the total and check the price breakdown rather than adding fees twice.

A 2- or 3-night allowance does not mean the stay can be booked. On 14 October 2026, Seven Mile Beach showed no online availability for both options; Shoalhaven Heads displayed Meroo at $280/night with a 7-night minimum. Ask providers about short-stay options.

05 / KEEP IT SIMPLE

Less organising.
More being there.

A loose rhythm for the 8–11 January option.
One booked activity is plenty.

  1. FRI

    Arrive & exhale

    Drive after lunch if possible. Unpack, explore the park, easy BBQ dinner.

  2. SAT

    Our big little adventure

    A surf lesson, dolphin cruise or family paddle. Afternoon pool time.

  3. SUN

    No alarms required

    Beach or lake, picnic lunch, then games and a big shared dinner.

  4. MON

    One last coffee

    Pack up for the property’s checkout time. Leave room for a stop on the way home.
